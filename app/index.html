    ⚠ One trophy may be missable. Positive Mental Attitude needs a meditation at every spot in Leonida — and the first one, Lucia’s balcony meditation at Jason’s house in Chapter 1, disappears when the chapter ends. There is no mission replay. Do it before you leave Chapter 1.

    Roadmap to the Platinum

    About 200 hours, difficulty 4 out of 10, one playthrough, no online trophies. Cheats switch trophies and saving off, so keep them off for the whole run. There is no mission replay and no New Game Plus — keep a manual save at the start of every chapter and one before the final mission.

    1. Step 1 — Saves and cheats. Turn cheats off, keep manual saves at every chapter start, and never rely on mission replay — it does not exist.
    2. Step 2 — Chapter 1: the balcony meditation. After the mission Down to Earth, talk to Lucia in the kitchen in the morning and follow her to the balcony. Meditate with her. This is the one interaction that can disappear for good.
    3. Step 3 — Chapters 2 to 4: scan and take the optional missions. Play the story, take Strangers & Freaks strands and side-character missions as they appear, and scan vehicles and study animals with the binoculars as you travel.
    4. Step 4 — Chapter 3: robbery tools. Robbery jobs open up here — shops, armored trucks and the first bank work. Learn the tools now; the vaults come next.
    5. Step 5 — Chapter 4: the vaults. Work through the bank vault jobs and the gang operations. Eight vaults exist; the checklist needs all of them.
    6. Step 6 — 100% cleanup after the credits. The checklist appears under Progress, then Stats, then 100% Completion. Work it in free roam: robberies, fencing totals, collectibles and activities.
    7. Step 7 — Final sweep. Finish the last challenges, the remaining card sets and WAiNK spots, the last stunts and races, and the Platinum pops with A Long Road.
    100% completion checklist — the numbers A Long Road needs
    • 100 optional tasks
    • Robberies: 39 gang operations, 22 bank jobs, 7 payday jobs, 34 of 35 smash’n’grabs, 50 of 91 stick ups, 8 of 8 vaults
    • Fencing: $100,000 narcotics, $100,000 jewelry, $250,000 marked bills, $200,000 vehicles
    • Collectibles: 15 of 18 card sets, 100 WAiNK spots, 33 underwater finds, 15 of 18 meditations, 100 of 125 flamingos
    • Scanning: 200 of 277 vehicles, 50 of 66 weapons, 100 of 255 animals
    • Activities: 30 of 40 races, gold in all 42 shooting-range challenges, 42 drone trials, 300 of 397 stunts, 70 of 80 challenges, 15 of 15 Bret target decks

    Counts from PowerPyx and KeenGamer, who agree on the checklist. Progress made during the story already counts toward it.
